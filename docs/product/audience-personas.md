## 2. Deep Persona Profiles

### Persona 01: The Emergent Lagos Creator
* **Name:** Tayo Adeleke  
* **Demographics:** 24, Visual Designer & Brand Strategist, Lagos (Yaba/Lekki)  
* **Mindset:** "Western design history gave me Bauhaus and Swiss Modernism; where is the canon of Nigerian graphic identity?"  
* **Motivations:**
  - Finding authentic local references for commercial design, album artwork, and streetwear brand identity.
  - Frustrated by generic "tribal patterns" and vector mudcloth clichés found on stock asset sites.
  - Seeking original typographic specimens, post-independence print ephemera, and record sleeve design systems.
* **Core Needs from ARCHIVE:**
  - Bold, impactful visual presentation on mobile (iPhone 14 / Safari).
  - High-res inspection of layout techniques, hand-lettered posters, and print textures.
  - Direct attribution and creator names to cite in pitch decks and creative treatments.
* **Key Friction Points:**
  - Academic jargon, sluggish load times on cellular connections, paywalled collections.

---

### Persona 02: The Diaspora Reconnector
* **Name:** Amina Okonjo-Burt  
* **Demographics:** 31, Fashion Stylist & Creative Director, London (Peckham/Hackney)  
* **Mindset:** "My parents brought highlife records and family photo albums to the UK in the 1980s. I want to understand the creative ecosystem that gave birth to that dignity."  
* **Motivations:**
  - Connecting personal familial memory with institutional documentation.
  - Deepening research for editorial fashion shoots referencing Lagos 1970s silhouettes, gele architecture, and agbada drape.
  - Experiencing the dialogue between vintage tailoring and current London-Lagos contemporary streetwear.
* **Core Needs from ARCHIVE:**
  - Rich narrative context connecting the artifact to its social environment (e.g., Sunday portrait studios in Surulere).
  - Seamless audio and visual pairings that convey atmosphere and mood.
  - Clear sharing mechanics to post curated archive cards to Instagram and Are.na.
* **Key Friction Points:**
  - Superficial nostalgia without historical substance; lack of information on textiles and dyes.

---

### Persona 03: The Specialist Practitioner & Cultural Researcher
* **Name:** Dr. Chike Obi  
* **Demographics:** 46, Architectural Historian & University Lecturer, Ibadan / Berlin  
* **Mindset:** "Preservation without provenance is folklore. Digital archives must respect attribution, date veracity, and regional specificity."  
* **Motivations:**
  - Sourcing primary archival artifacts for lectures, exhibition catalogues, and academic publications.
  - Verifying dates, studio locations, and copyright ownership for mid-20th-century Nigerian photography.
  - Analyzing how political movements (e.g., FESTAC '77, Independence 1960) manifested across physical print and fabric.
* **Core Needs from ARCHIVE:**
  - Standardized cataloging metadata (Accession ID, Creator, Medium, Date, Provenance, Rights status).
  - Multi-faceted filtering by decade, medium, cultural origin, and discipline.
  - Deep research bibliography and citations.
* **Key Friction Points:**
  - Unverifiable claims, fuzzy or uncaptioned photos, lack of rights transparency, decorative-only UI animations.

---

### Persona 04: The Global Contemporary Culture Enthusiast
* **Name:** Marcus Lindqvist  
* **Demographics:** 28, Music Producer & Digital Culture Enthusiast, Stockholm  
* **Mindset:** "I love contemporary Afrobeats (Burna Boy, Wizkid, Asake), but what is the sonic and visual lineage behind this explosion?"  
* **Motivations:**
  - Tracing modern African pop culture back to highlife, fuji, jùjú, and Fela Kuti's Afrobeat.
  - Experiencing a cutting-edge digital museum that rivals the best European cultural web experiences (e.g., Stedelijk, Tate, Centre Pompidou).
* **Core Needs from ARCHIVE:**
  - Accessible, frictionless entry point with immediate visual payoff.
  - Engaging "Through the Years" timeline that demystifies eras and sonic evolutions.
  - Audio clips paired with historical sleeve art to appreciate sound and vision simultaneously.
* **Key Friction Points:**
  - Cluttered navigation, obscure insider terminology without tooltips, slow or unresponsive interactions.
