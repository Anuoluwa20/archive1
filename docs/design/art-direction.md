## 1. Visual Statement & Creative Philosophy

ARCHIVE exists at the threshold of physical preservation and urgent digital celebration. Our aesthetic approach is defined by **tactile editorial dignity**, **uncompromising typographic scale**, and **deliberate curatorial restraint**. 

Every digital artifact is treated with the care of an unboxed physical specimen in a museum conservation lab, yet charged with the contemporary graphic confidence of a leading Lagos art publication.

## 2. Core Visual Pillars

### 2.1 The Monumental & The Micro
We pair colossal condensed display typography (that asserts cultural permanence) directly against razor-sharp, microscopic cataloging metadata (which establishes institutional authority). This dynamic scale shift creates immediate visual tension and eliminates bland middle-ground layouts.

### 2.2 Archival Materiality Without Skewing Retro
We reject cheesy vintage faux-distress filters. Instead, we reference the structural components of physical curation:
- **Contact Sheet Strip Grids:** Uniform horizontal sequences with frame numbers (`ARC-TX-04`, `ARC-TX-05`).
- **Accession Badges & Colophons:** Sharp rectangular bordered pills containing catalog IDs (`ARC-PH-1974-001`).
- **Tactile Paper Tones:** Subtle off-white vellum surfaces (`#F7F4EE`) that soften harsh digital screens.
- **Red Archival Ink:** Accents in oxidized lead/terracotta (`#A33B26`) evoking official ink stamps and conservation wax seals.

### 2.3 Distinct Discipline Signatures
To celebrate the four core disciplines without fracturing brand unity, each is granted a distinct yet harmonious signature:

| Discipline | Curatorial Colorway | Visual Metaphor | Typographic Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01 PHOTOGRAPHY** | Silver Gelatin Carbon (`#111111`) & Fiber Cream (`#F0EBE1`) | Darkroom contact sheets, crop grease marks, negative margins | Chiseled, deliberate, silent |
| **02 MUSIC** | Vinyl Obsidian (`#0F1115`) & Sunburst Ochre (`#D97706`) | LP circular center labels, sound wave grooves, cassette J-cards | Polyrhythmic, vibrating, kinetic |
| **03 FASHION** | Indigo Vat Deep (`#141E28`) & Raw Sányán Silk (`#D4B996`) | Warp and weft loom grids, structural seamlines, drape folds | Sculptural, tactile, luxurious |
| **04 DESIGN** | Letterpress Black (`#18181B`) & Oxidized Terracotta (`#9E3D22`) | Specimen sheets, woodblock registration crosses, broadsheets | Bold, architectural, structural |

## 3. Image Cropping & Framing Rules

1. **Never Crop Original Studio Scans:**
   When presenting vintage studio portraits (e.g., J.D. Ojeikere, Peter Obe, Tam Fiofori), maintain the full image frame including original border marks, film manufacturer margins, and edge perforations.
2. **Asymmetric Horizon Alignment:**
   In editorial split layouts, offset image baselines against text baselines to create dynamic visual rhythm.
3. **Meaningful Negative Space:**
   Surround monumental headline statements with generous margins (at least 20% of viewport width on desktop) to allow the text to resonate like a spoken proclamation.
