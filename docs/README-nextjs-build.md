## ≡ƒÅ¢∩╕Å Executive Vision & Creative Proposition

**ARCHIVE** exists at the meeting point of a cultural institution, an experimental editorial magazine, a living historical archive, and an immersive digital exhibition. 

Its foundational premise is simple and uncompromising: **The past is not past.** The radical creative choices made by earlier generationsΓÇöfrom post-independence studio photographers in Lagos and highlife brass big bands to anti-colonial letterpress pamphleteers and architectural textile weaversΓÇöremain living, active catalysts for contemporary global culture.

## ≡ƒô£ Complete 9-Section Landing Page Architecture

1. **01 ΓÇö Opening Statement (Hero):** Monumental display headline (*"THE PAST IS NOT PAST"*), live Lagos time (WAT / UTC+1) status rail, accession count (`510+ Verified References`), and dual CTAs (*ENTER THE ARCHIVE*, *EXPLORE COLLECTION*).
2. **02 ΓÇö Editorial Manifesto:** Poetic curatorial proclamation (*"Before it became a movement, it was a moment. A photograph. A record. A garment. A mark on paper."*) and quotes from Fela Kuti, J.D. Ojeikere, and Shade Thomas-Fahm.
3. **03 ΓÇö Four Disciplines Matrix:** Interactive matrix exploring **Photography**, **Music**, **Fashion**, and **Design** with Yoruba terminology, seminal pioneers, key movements, and direct filter actions.
4. **04 ΓÇö Featured Cultural Story:** Monograph study on **J.D. 'Okhai Ojeikere: Architecture of the Crown (1968ΓÇô1999)** with interactive 125% zoom loupe, hairstyle typology specimens (*Onile Gogoro*, *Patewo*, *Suku*, *Kiko*), oral history narration, and verified provenance.

6. **06 ΓÇö The Living Collection:** Faceted archival grid featuring real-time client-side search across 510+ records, discipline pills, era dropdowns, and interactive **Curatorial Lightbox Drawers** with accession ledgers and citation copying.
7. **07 ΓÇö The Continuing Story:** Interactive touch-enabled comparison slider juxtaposing vintage masters (*Rex Lawson, Shade Thomas-Fahm, J.D. Ojeikere, FESTAC '77*) with modern creators (*The Cavemen, Kenneth Ize, Stephen Tayo, digital type studios*).
8. **08 ΓÇö Closing Invitation:** Ongoing legacy portal featuring an interactive **Archival Intake Portal** for public ephemera submission, the **Curatorial Dispatch** subscription, and a live **Visitor Book**.
9. **09 ΓÇö Institutional Footer:** Full institutional directory, statutory Nigerian archive links (NCMM, National Archives, HSPACA, Digital Benin), live Lagos clock, and back-to-top trigger.
